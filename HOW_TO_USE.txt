HOW TO USE: cartesia-accent-translator
====================================

PURPOSE
  Translate quotes, dialogue, phrases, or word lists into authentic accented
  English phonetics using Cartesia's canonical broad phonemic IPA syntax
  (<<ˈ|m|ɝ|d|ə|ˈ|b|i|ə|t|s>>). Delivers in-chat inline TTS-ready prompts and
  copy-pasteable dictionary snippets without writing files to disk.

CAPABILITIES
  - In-chat, zero-disk on-demand phonetic translation
  - Extensive accent library: East Asian, European, South Asian, Regional Anglophone, Fictional/Persona
  - Strict Cartesia pipe-separated broad phonemic IPA (<<...>>)
  - Isolated stress tokens (|ˈ|, |ˌ|) before stressed syllables
  - Atomic diphthong & affricate protection (|aɪ|, |eɪ|, |oʊ|, |tʃ|, |dʒ|)
  - Vowel elongation and cadence modulation (|l|aɪ|aɪ|k|)
  - Inline TTS text + word-by-word phonetics breakdown table + JSON snippet

USE CASES
  - Convert dialogue line into Scottish, French, German, or Russian accent for Cartesia Sonic
  - Build custom pronunciation dictionary entries for regional character speech
  - Generate phonetically authentic non-native English line reads
  - Test vowel drawls or non-native cadence in voice synthesis

IDEAS
  - Provide a dialogue line and ask for 3 distinct regional accent variations
  - Combine with cartesia-voice-clone-extractor for complete accent-cloned personas
  - Translate technical jargon into a specific localized dialect

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  /cartesia-accent-translator

  Translate to French accent: "The meeting starts at seven o'clock tonight."

  How would a Scottish speaker say: "I told you we cannot do that right now."

  Accent translation for: "Welcome to our laboratory, my dear friend." in Russian accent.

  Give me the Cartesia phonetics for "water bottle" in British Cockney.
